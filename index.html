BlueCollar-Bench

A benchmark and open egocentric dataset of repeated physical and industrial work cycles, for evaluating prediction, failure, and generalization in physical AI.

Science lead: Patrick Rim (Build AI Research) · Process & ops: Midcentury

GitHub org · Site source

Abstract

Progress in physical AI depends on measuring how models behave on real work, not only on scripted lab tasks. BlueCollar-Bench is a collaborative multi-lab effort to build a shared benchmark and open egocentric dataset of skilled, repeated work cycles captured across workers, stations, sites, and countries. The goal is a durable community resource for studying prediction, failure, and generalization under the structure of industrial and physical labor.

Overview

The project centers on egocentric video of people performing repeated physical work cycles. That structure supports evaluation along axes that matter for physical AI: predicting what happens next in a cycle, anticipating failure, and generalizing across workers, stations, sites, countries, and industries.

Track definitions, metrics, and splits are being settled with the collaborator group toward a planning freeze in early October 2026, with the paper targeting ICML 2027.

Dataset

Alongside the benchmark, the project plans a joint release of a large open egocentric dataset of skilled physical work. Project framing for the broader corpus includes on the order of hundreds of thousands of individuals and many thousands of capture settings; the public split and evaluation protocol will be fixed with the collaborator group before scores are published.
